# Air Fryer Salmon with Lemon-Yogurt Sauce

## Ingredients

- 2 salmon fillets
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt (full or 2%)
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1 garlic clove, grated or minced
- 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1/4 tsp salt (for the sauce)

## Directions

1. Pat the salmon very dry with paper towels. Rub all over with the olive oil, then season with the salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
2. Air fry at 390 °F for 8 to 10 minutes depending on thickness, no flip needed. Pull at 125 to 130 °F internal for moist medium; it carries up a few degrees as it rests.
3. While the salmon cooks, stir together the Greek yogurt, lemon zest and juice, grated garlic, dill, and salt. Taste and adjust — more lemon for brightness, more salt if flat. Loosen with a splash of water if you want it more drizzle-able.
4. Let the salmon rest 2 to 3 minutes off the heat, then spoon the lemon-yogurt sauce over the top or pool it underneath. Serve alongside the caprese and pesto quinoa.

## Notes

If you don't have fresh dill, dried works in a pinch (use about 1 tsp) — or skip it and lean on the lemon and garlic. The sauce keeps a couple days in the fridge and is great on roasted veg or as a dip later.
