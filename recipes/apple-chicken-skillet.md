# Apple Chicken Skillet

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 honeycrisp apples, cored, peeled, and cut into 1/4" wedges
- 1 lb boneless chicken thighs
- 1/2 onion thinly sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1/2 cp heavy cream
- 1/4 cp white wine
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried rosemary
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 pinch ground allspice
- 1 tsp dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp pure maple syrup

## Directions

1. Into a bowl, coat the chicken in an [[All Purpose Dredge for Chicken]]. Heat oil in a skillet on medium high heat and cook chicken through (about ~5-8 min/side), then remove and set aside
2. Add onions and cook until softened, stirring occasionally (~5 min)
3. Add 2 tbsp butter, garlic, thyme, rosemary, allspice, and cinnamon. Cook until fragrant
4. Deglaze with white wine, let simmer until reduced by half
5. Add cream, dijon, remainder of butter, and maple syrup. Stir, then add apples and return chicken to pan to simmer for ~5 minutes
6. Finish with a pinch salt to taste
