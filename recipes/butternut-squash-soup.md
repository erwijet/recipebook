# Butternut Squash Soup

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 shallots, diced
- 1 tbsp fresh sage, finely chopped
- 1 sprig fresh rosemary
- 3 lbs butternut squash, peeled and cut into 1" cubes
- 4 cp chicken broth

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 325F. Toss butternut squash with oil and salt generously, then bake while chicken cooks, appx 20 minutes.
2. Add 1 tbsp oil to pot over medium heat. Sear chicken until just cooked (you can technically go a little under, but I get anxious). Remove and let rest.
3. Add 1 tbsp more oil to pot, add shallots and cook until softened, then add the herbs and cook for appx 30 seconds.
4. Deglaze with 1/4 cp of stock, then add the rest of the stock, and add cubed butternut squash.
5. Bring to a boil, then let simmer until the squash is softened and can be squished with a fork, appx 20-25 minutes.
6. Remove rosemary sprig, mash half-ish of the squash, and stir to thicken. Cube and add chicken back in. Simmer 5-7 more minutes, then serve with fresh cracked black pepper and heavy cream to taste.
