# Chipotle Crema

## Ingredients

- 1.5 cp plain greek yogurt (350g)
- 4 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced (63g)
- 2 tbsp adobo sauce from the can (33g)
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 1.5 limes, juiced
- 1 lime's worth of zest
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp salt

## Directions

1. Combine the ingredients and whisk together.

## Notes

Keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for a week.
