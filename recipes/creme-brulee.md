## Ingredients

- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
- 100g granulated sugar
- 5 egg yolks
- 1/8 tsp salt

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 325 °F.
2. Combine the cream, vanilla, and salt in a saucepan until hot.
3. Beat the egg yolks and sugar in a bowl until light.
4. Slowly pour the hot cream over the egg yolk mixture while stirring continuously.
5. Pour the mixture into ramekins and place the ramekins in a baking dish.
6. Fill the dish with boiling water halfway up the sides of the ramekins.
7. Bake for 30 to 40 minutes.
