# Easy Potato Soup

![Easy Potato Soup](assets/easy-potato-soup.jpg)

A quick and easy potato soup using 4 simple ingredients.

## Ingredients

- 4 leeks sliced, or 2 medium/large onions diced
- 6 medium sized potatoes, yielding about 6 cups diced potatoes
- 6 cups of broth (chicken or veggie)
- 1 8oz block of cream cheese
- olive oil, salt & pepper
- toppings of choice (bacon, scallions, cheese etc.)

## Directions

1. In a large dutch oven or stock pot, heat some olive oil over medium heat.
2. Add the leeks or onions and cook until soft and just starting to brown. Add salt & pepper.
3. Add the potatoes and enough broth to just cover them. (Save the rest for later.)
4. Bring to a boil and cook until the potatoes are soft. (Test with a fork — if it goes through easily they are ready.)
5. Using a slotted spoon, remove some of the potatoes and set aside.
6. Add the softened cream cheese in pieces and purée with an immersion blender until smooth. Add the rest of the potatoes back in and season with more salt & pepper to taste. Add more broth if desired depending on how thick you want your soup, and simmer for 5-10 minutes.
7. Top with grated cheese, scallions, bacon, herbs, or other toppings of choice.
