# Feb 3 Magic Night Chicken Katsu with Quick Pickled Cucumber and Miso Soup

## Ingredients

### Chicken katsu

- 4 chicken breasts
- 8 tbsp olive oil
- plenty of panko
- 3 eggs
- 1/2 cp flour
- 1/2 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- pinch msg
- kosher salt

### Miso soup

- 8 cp water
- 4 dashi stock packets
- 1 tsp dried wakame
- 2 tbsp finely sliced green onion
- 1/4 cp cubed firm tofu
- 6 tbsp white miso

### Tonkatsu sauce

- 4 tbsp ketchup
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 tsp ground ginger
- 2 cloves minced garlic
- 1 tsp honey (optional)

### Pickled cucumber

- 1 seeded cucumber, finely sliced
- 1/4 cp seasoned rice vinegar
- 1/4 cp champagne wine vinegar
- 1 tsp fresh dill, finely chopped

### Rice

- 2 cp jasmine rice
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- kosher salt

## Directions

1. Begin the rice as it takes forever to cook. Use a rice cooker: 2 cp jasmine rice to 2.5 cp water. Once it's done, add the sesame oil and salt to taste.
2. Thinly slice the cucumber and place in a glass container with the seasoned rice vinegar, champagne wine vinegar, and dill for at least 10 minutes. Be sure it's fully mixed and incorporated.
3. Meanwhile, combine the garlic and onion powder with the paprika and a sprinkle of msg with the flour on a plate or tin foil.
4. Whisk 3 eggs in a small bowl with a splash of water.
5. On a sheet of tin foil, dump a bunch of panko.
6. Pound the chicken breasts into 1/2 in thick cutlets. Ensure all are the same size. Coat fully in the seasoned flour, then egg, then panko.
7. Preheat 2 tbsp of olive oil per breast, then pan fry each side ~3 minutes. Remove any bits of panko between, adding more oil as needed. Once finished, sprinkle with kosher salt and set aside.
8. While cooking the katsu, bring 8 cp water to a simmer but not a boil. Add 4 dashi packets (or whatever the ratio for the specific packet is) and steep for 3 minutes. Remove and discard the packets.
9. Add the wakame. Be sure to break it into very tiny pieces as it will expand nearly 10x in the soup.
10. Cut the heat to 0, then add the white miso and stir until combined. Add the tofu and sliced green onion.
11. Cover with a lid, but make sure to keep the heat off.
12. Combine the ketchup, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, ginger, and garlic in a small bowl and whisk until combined. Optionally, add honey to taste.
13. Serve with a bed of rice over half the plate, the cutlet draped over with a spread of the sauce across the top. Finish with additional thinly sliced green onion. Place the pickled cucumber next to the rice, and the miso soup in a small bowl.
