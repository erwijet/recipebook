# Feta & Spinach Stuffed Chicken Breast

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 1 cup fresh spinach, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup crumbled feta cheese
- 1 tablespoon olive oil, for the filling
- 1.5 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 0.5 teaspoons red pepper flakes (optional)
- 0.5 teaspoons salt
- 0.3 teaspoons black pepper
- 1 tablespoon olive oil, for brushing the chicken

## Directions

1. Sauté the garlic and spinach: Heat the olive oil for the filling in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the garlic for 3 minutes until fragrant, then add the spinach and cook until wilted.
2. Mix the stuffing: Remove from the heat and let cool slightly. Mix in the feta, about 1 tsp of the oregano, the lemon juice, and the red pepper flakes if using.
3. Cut pockets in the chicken: Slice each breast horizontally to create a pocket, being careful not to cut all the way through.
4. Stuff and season: Stuff each breast with the feta-spinach mixture and secure with toothpicks. Brush the outside with the olive oil for brushing and season with the salt, black pepper, and remaining oregano.
5. Preheat the air fryer to 375 °F (190 °C).
6. Air fry the chicken: Arrange the stuffed breasts in the basket in a single layer, not touching. Work in batches if needed. Air fry at 375 °F (190 °C) for 20 minutes, flipping halfway through, until the internal temperature reaches 165 °F (74 °C).
7. Rest and serve: Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes, then remove the toothpicks before serving.

## Notes

Because there's no stovetop sear in this version, brushing the outside with oil before air frying is what gets you a browned, slightly crisp exterior. Basket size varies a lot between models — if the breasts are thick or your basket is small, cook in two batches rather than crowding them, and check the temperature with a meat thermometer at the thickest part. Avoid probing into the filling pocket, which reads hotter. If your air fryer runs hot, start checking the temperature at the 16-minute mark.
