# Honey Garlic Chicken Alfredo

## Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1/4 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp white wine
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1/4 cup parmesan, grated
- 1 tsp each: honey, soy sauce, and flour
- 2 oz pasta (penne, rigatoni, etc)

## Directions

1. cook 2 oz pasta in salted water. reserve ~1/4 cup of pasta water
2. add chicken thighs to a cold pan over medium high heat. cook until cooked through and crispy. set aside
3. add 1/4 onion and 2 cloves garlic. cook until tender
4. deglaze with 2 tbsp white wine and let reduce until mostly gone
5. stir in 1 tsp each flour, honey, and soy sauce and cook about 30 seconds
6. stir in 1/4 cup heavy cream and simmer briefly until slightly thickened. turn heat to low, then stir in 1/4 cup parm
7. add cooked pasta and toss, adding pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce coats the pasta
8. plate and finish with a pinch of parm and pepper. top with chicken
