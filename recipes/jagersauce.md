# Jägersauce

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 0.5 tablespoons neutral oil
- 150 grams cremini or button mushrooms, sliced
- 0.5 shallot (or small onion), finely diced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 0.5 tablespoons tomato paste
- 0.5 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 60 milliliters dry white wine (or red)
- 150 milliliters beef stock
- 60 milliliters heavy cream
- 0.5 teaspoons Dijon mustard
- 0.3 teaspoons sweet paprika
- 0.3 teaspoons salt
- 0.1 teaspoons black pepper
- 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped

## Directions

1. Brown the mushrooms: Heat the butter and oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and cook, stirring only occasionally, until they've released their water and turned golden brown. Don't rush this — real color here is where the flavor lives.
2. Add aromatics: Add the shallot and garlic. Cook until the shallot is soft and translucent.
3. Build the base: Stir in the tomato paste and flour and cook for a minute to toast off the raw flour and deepen the tomato paste.
4. Deglaze: Pour in the wine, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Let it reduce by about half.
5. Simmer: Add the beef stock and bring to a gentle simmer. Let it reduce and thicken slightly.
6. Finish: Lower the heat and stir in the heavy cream, Dijon mustard, and sweet paprika. Simmer another couple of minutes, then season with the salt and black pepper. Stir in the parsley right before serving and spoon generously over your schnitzel.
