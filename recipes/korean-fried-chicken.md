# Korean Fried Chicken

## Ingredients

- 0.5 cp soy sauce
- 0.25 cp rice wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp msg
- splash of sesame oil
- cornstarch
- vegetable oil
- 1 lb chicken tenderloin

## Directions

1. Cut chicken tenderloin into 1-2in pieces, and add to a bowl with the soy sauce, vinegar, and sesame oil. Toss to coat, and let marinade for 30 minutes.
2. Into a large bowl, combine lots of cornstarch (around 2 cp), garlic powder, and msg. Whisk briefly to incorporate.
3. Heat 2-3" of oil in a pot on the stove to 350F. In batches, toss the marinated chicken in cornstarch, then lower into the oil. Cook around 4 minutes, and remove to a wire rack with paper towels underneath. Salt immediately.
4. Toss with a [[Toknkatsu Sauce]] and serve over a bed of jasmine rice.
