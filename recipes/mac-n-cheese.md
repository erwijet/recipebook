# Mac n Cheese

## Ingredients

- 1 lb elbow macaroni pasta, uncooked
- 1 cp evaporated milk
- 1.5 cp half & half
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 tbsp AP flour
- 4 oz cream cheese
- 8 oz gouda cheese, cubed
- 8 oz creamy havarti, cubed
- 8 oz sharp cheddar cheese, cubed
- 4 oz mozzarella cheese (shredded by hand)
- 4 oz colby jack cheese (shredded by hand)
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 0.5 tsp fresh cracked pepper
- 1 tsp smoked paprika

## Directions

1. Pour 8 cps water into a pot and sprinkle in 1 tsp salt.
2. Bring the water to a boil over high heat, then add the elbow macaroni.
3. Cook the pasta until al dente, then drain and rinse under cool water.
4. Place a large sauce pan over medium heat with 4 tbsp butter.
5. Melt the butter and sprinkle in 2 tbsp of flour.
6. Whisk until well incorporated, then pour in the evaporated milk and half & half.
7. Whisk and continue to cook over medium heat for about 3 minutes.
8. Reduce the heat to low, add the cream cheese, gouda, and havarti. Stir until creamy.
9. Sprinkle in 1 tsp salt, pepper, onion powder, and garlic powder. Mix until combined.
10. Toss the macaroni into a large pot and pour in the cheese sauce.
11. Stir until well combined, then pour half of the macaroni and cheese into a 9x13 bake dish.
12. Sprinkle some of the sharp cheddar, mozzarella, and colby jack on top.
13. Add the remaining mac and cheese to the bake dish, and top off with the remaining cheese.
14. Bake in an oven preheated to 350 °F for 25-30 min.
15. Remove from the oven and let sit for 5-10 minutes.
