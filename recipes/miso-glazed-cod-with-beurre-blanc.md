# Miso Glazed Cod with Beurre Blanc & Baby Bok Choy

## Ingredients

### Miso glaze

- 3 tbsp white miso
- 0.25 cp mirin
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 0.5 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 0.5 tbsp ginger
- 2 cod fillets (6oz)

### Beurre blanc

- 9 tbsp butter
- 1 minced shallot
- 1/4 cp dry white wine
- 0.5 lemon's worth of juice

### Baby bok choy

- 2 heads baby bok choy, quartered lengthwise
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves minced garlic

### Other

- dry roasted peanuts
- scallions

## Directions

1. Add the miso glaze ingredients together in a small sauce pan and bring to a simmer over medium high heat, but do not let it boil. Remove from the heat and let cool to room temp.
2. Place the cod on a plate and brush with the miso glaze, then refrigerate for 30 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, heat 1 tbsp of butter in a small saucepan over medium high heat. Add the shallots, then cook for 2-3 min stirring frequently.
4. Add the wine, bring to a boil, and allow to reduce for 3 min.
5. Begin to add the butter 1 tbsp at a time, allowing each to melt before adding another. Whisk constantly.
6. Once thick and creamy, remove from the heat.
7. Whisk in the citrus juice and 1/4 tsp kosher salt to taste.
8. Preheat the broiler for 3 minutes and then broil the cod for 7-10 minutes until the exterior is caramelized.
9. While the fish is cooking, prepare the bok choy. Heat the oil in a pan over medium high heat, add the garlic, and cook until fragrant.
10. Add the bok choy cut side down with a pinch of salt. Cook each side for a minute, then remove.
11. Warm the beurre blanc again and whisk over medium heat until fluid, then ladle over the plate. Add the bok choy and cod on top with crushed peanuts and finely sliced scallions.
