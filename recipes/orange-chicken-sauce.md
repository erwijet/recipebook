# Orange Chicken Sauce

## Ingredients

- 0.5 orange, juiced
- 1 tsp orange zest
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 2 tsp cornstarch (mixed with 2 tbsp water)

## Directions

1. Heat saucepan over medium heat. Add garlic and ginger for 30 seconds
2. Pour in soy sauce, orange juice, orange zest, honey, and rice wine vinegar. Whisk and let simmer 3 min
3. Slowly pour in cornstarch slurry
4. Cook until thickened
