# Pesto

## Ingredients

- 0.5 cups extra virgin olive oil
- 2 lemons, juiced
- 2 cups fresh basil leaves, packed
- 0.3 cups pine nuts (or walnuts/almonds)
- 0.5 cups Parmesan, finely grated
- 2 garlic cloves
- 0.5 teaspoons salt

## Directions

1. Toast the nuts: Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan over medium heat for 3-4 minutes, shaking often, until fragrant and lightly golden. This buys back a lot of flavor depth the bullet's smooth blend would otherwise flatten. Let them cool.
2. Oil goes in first: Pour the olive oil and the lemon juice into the NutriBullet cup FIRST. Counterintuitive, but the thick paste needs the oil down by the blade to form a vortex, otherwise it spins above the blade and never catches.
3. Layer the rest on top: Add the cooled nuts, then the garlic, Parmesan, and salt, and finally pack the basil on top. Don't fill past two-thirds — things need room to move.
4. Pulse, don't blitz: Twist-lock and run in short bursts rather than continuously. Shake or invert the cup between bursts to redistribute. Short pulses keep the blade from heating the basil, which is what turns pesto muddy-brown and bitter.
5. Adjust and finish: If it's stubbornly not catching, add olive oil a tablespoon at a time until it pulls into the vortex. Blend just until it's a coarse paste — the bullet trends toward uniform purée, so stop before it goes totally smooth if you want any texture. Taste and adjust the salt.
