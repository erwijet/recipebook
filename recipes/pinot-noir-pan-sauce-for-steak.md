# Pinot Noir Pan Sauce for Steak

## Ingredients

- 0.5 shallot, finely minced
- 1 garlic clove, smashed
- 0.5 cups pinot noir
- 0.5 cups beef or veal stock, low sodium
- 1.5 fresh thyme sprigs
- 0.5 teaspoons Dijon mustard
- 3 tablespoons cold unsalted butter, cubed
- 0.1 teaspoons black pepper, freshly cracked
- dash of sherry vinegar or balsamic

## Directions

1. Sweat the shallot, then add the garlic to the steak pan. Cook until aromatic.
2. Deglaze with the pinot noir and reduce until syrupy.
3. Add the beef stock and thyme. Reduce until it coats the back of a spoon.
4. Remove from the heat and mount with the butter.
5. Season with the vinegar, Dijon, and pepper.
