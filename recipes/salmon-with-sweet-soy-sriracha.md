# Salmon with Sweet Soy Sriracha

## Ingredients

- 1 portion salmon
- 3 cloves minced garlic
- 1/2 tsp ginger
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- honey to taste
- sriracha to taste

## Directions

1. Cook the salmon and let it rest.
2. Add the ginger and garlic to the pan over medium high heat, cooking for 30ish seconds until browned.
3. Add the soy sauce and stir, then add the honey (~1 tsp-ish) and a little bit of sriracha.
4. Taste and adjust the honey and sriracha accordingly.
5. Don't let it over-reduce. Serve with the salmon as soon as it's balanced.
