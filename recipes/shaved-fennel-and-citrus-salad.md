# Shaved Fennel and Citrus Salad

## Ingredients

- 1 fennel bulb, cored
- 1 navel orange (or blood orange)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 0.5 teaspoons salt
- 0.3 teaspoons black pepper
- 2 tablespoons fennel fronds, chopped
- 2 tablespoons shaved parmesan or crumbled feta

## Directions

1. Shave and crisp the fennel: Shave the fennel as thin as possible, ideally on a mandoline. Drop the slices into a bowl of ice water for 10 minutes to make them extra crisp, then drain and pat dry. Save the chopped fronds for garnish.
2. Supreme the orange: Cut the top and bottom off the orange, stand it up, and slice away the peel and pith. Cut into rounds or segment it, working over a bowl to catch the juice.
3. Make the dressing: Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, any caught orange juice, salt, and black pepper together until emulsified.
4. Assemble: Toss the fennel with just enough dressing to coat. Arrange with the orange, scatter the parmesan and reserved fennel fronds over the top, and finish with a crack of pepper.

## Notes

Keeps 2 days.
