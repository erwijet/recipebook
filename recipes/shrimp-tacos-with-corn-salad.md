# Shrimp Tacos with Corn Salad

## Ingredients

### Shrimp

- 1 lb peeled deveined shrimp
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 0.5 tsp garlic powder
- 0.5 tsp onion powder
- 0.5 tsp smoked paprika
- 0.5 tsp cumin
- 0.5 tsp chili powder
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.25 tsp ground black pepper
- 8 medium flour tortillas

### Crema

- 2 tbsp sour cream
- 2 tbsp mayo
- 1 clove of garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 0.25 cp fresh chopped cilantro
- 1 pinch of salt

### Slaw

- 1 cp thin sliced napa cabbage
- 0.5 lime, juice + zest
- 0.5 tsp olive oil
- 0.5 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1/4 tsp honey
- 1/4 tsp salt

### Toppings

- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1/4 cp cotija cheese
- 1/4 cp pickled red onion

### Corn salad

- 2 ears corn
- 1 tbsp butter
- 0.5 lime (juice + zest)
- 2 tbsp cotija cheese
- 1 tbsp mayo
- 1 tbsp sour cream
- 1 tbsp finely chopped cilantro
- 1 tbsp sliced green onion
- 0.5 tsp tajin

## Directions

1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the shrimp and spices and stir for around 1 minute to make sure they're evenly coated.
3. Cook around 5-6 minutes.
