# Veg Salad with Chicken

![Veg Salad with Chicken](assets/veg-salad-with-chicken.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 8 oz chicken breasts
- 0.5 cup GF baking flour
- 2 tbsp ap flour
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp mayo
- 0.5 avocado
- 4 cp romaine
- 0.5 tsp msg
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 0.5 cp cucumber
- 0.5 cp radish
- 0.25 cp honey crisp apple
- 0.25 cp pickled red onion
- 0.5 tsp dill
- 0.5 tsp parsley
- pinch mustard powder

## Directions

1. Add half the garlic powder, onion powder, msg, salt, and pepper (to taste) to the flour and half to the chicken directly.
2. Toss the chicken in a bag with the seasoned flour.
3. Add the olive oil to a pan and preheat, then cook the chicken.
4. While the chicken cooks, add the mayo, dill, parsley, mustard powder, salt, and pepper to a small dish and whisk to make the dressing.
5. Chop the romaine and cucumber. Finely dice the apple, and thinly slice the radishes.
6. Combine.
