# White Bean Puree

## Ingredients

- 1 15 oz can of cannellini beans
- 0.5 tsp garlic powder
- 0.25 cp dry white wine
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1/2 lemon's worth of juice
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/3 tsp pepper
- 0.5 shallot, diced
- olive oil

## Directions

1. Heat a bit of olive oil in a pan. Add the shallots and let them sweat until aromatic.
2. Add the wine and stir. Cook until it reduces slightly.
3. Add the beans, drained and rinsed, and the garlic powder. Simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Transfer to a blender, add the butter, and blitz until smooth. Add oil as needed to loosen.
5. Strain through a sieve and keep warm until serving.
